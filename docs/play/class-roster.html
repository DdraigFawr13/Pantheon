Caldrenne class roster

Revised with your changes: Wager-bound cut; an Elementalist, two wilderness and two craft classes added. Twenty-two base classes, halfway between the current eight and the familiar fantasy roles. Each keeps one signature trait and a primary and secondary stat. The "echoes" line says which classic role it borrows from; the names and traits are Caldrenne's own.

Front line

SellswordVigor / Agilitywas Blade-sworn

The trained fighter. Good with any weapon, best with one, and steadier the longer a fight runs.

Momentum: each successful exchange adds +5 to the next attack, up to +15.

Echoes: fighter

ReaverVigor / Resolvenew

Rage in armour. Gets more dangerous the more they bleed.

Blood Up: below half HP, +10 on attacks and −10 on defence until the fight ends.

Echoes: barbarian

WardenResolve / Vigorkept

The shield-bearer. Stands in front of others and takes the hit so they don't have to.

Bulwark: may take a blow aimed at an ally within reach.

Echoes: fighter (defender)

OathknightResolve / Presencenew

The holy warrior. Fights for a god's Oath and draws strength from keeping it.

Sworn Edge: +10 on checks that honour an Oath's Burden; may spend mana to heal an ally by touch.

Echoes: paladin

FistswornAgility / Resolvenew

Discipline instead of armour. Fast hands, a still mind, a body trained into a weapon.

Still Centre: unarmoured, defends with Agility and Resolve; stamina skills cost 20 % less.

Echoes: monk

SpellbladeVigor / Intellectnew

Sword in one hand, spell in the other. Hits with both at once.

Edgecasting: casts a spell through a weapon strike in the same exchange.

Echoes: magus

Skill and shadow

CutpurseAgility / Intellectnew

The thief and knife. Locks, pockets, rooftops and the first strike nobody saw coming.

Opening: +10 and double damage on the first strike against an unaware foe.

Echoes: rogue

WayfinderAgility / Resolvekept, stats changed

The scout and hunter. Never lost, always first to move, deadly at range.

Pathsense: never lost; acts before enemy intents resolve in the first exchange.

Echoes: ranger

InquestIntellect / Presencenew

The investigator. Reads people and scenes, and turns what they learn into an edge.

Studied Mark: after one exchange or scene observing a target, +10 on all checks against it.

Echoes: investigator

Magic

ArcanistIntellect / Resolvenew

The scholar-mage. Learns spells from study and has more of them than anyone.

Spellbook: one extra skill offered at each skill pick.

Echoes: wizard

ElementalistIntellect / Vigornew

Fire, frost, lightning, stone, wind or water, thrown hard. The classic battle-mage.

Attuned: choose Flame, Frost, Storm, Stone, Wind or Tide at awakening; spells of that element cost 20 % less mana and get +5.

Echoes: elementalist, evoker

WildbloodPresence / Vigornew

Magic in the blood, not the book. Raw, strong, and paid for in their own body.

Surge: may spend HP instead of mana to cast.

Echoes: sorcerer

CantorPresence / Intellectkept

The voice. Song and speech that steady friends, rattle enemies and talk a room round.

Carrying Voice: Song skills cost 20 % less mana and reach everyone who can hear.

Echoes: bard

VotaryResolve / Presencenew

A god's priest. Heals, wards and calls on a sworn god's favour.

Channel: healing and warding skills cost 20 % less; Divine Favour opens at Standing 50, not 60.

Echoes: cleric

HexerIntellect / Presencenew

Curses, bargains and quiet spite. Makes enemies weaker instead of themselves stronger.

Malediction: once per fight, curse one foe: −10 on all its checks.

Echoes: witch, warlock

GravewalkerResolve / Presencekept

The speaker for the dead. Talks with spirits and works death and ancestor magic.

Ancestral Voice: may question the spirits of the dead; Death and Spirit skills cost 20 % less.

Echoes: oracle, necromancer

Wilderness

WildkinVigor / Resolvekept, stats changed

The beast-bonded. Fights beside an animal companion that grows as they do.

Bonded Beast: a beast companion that levels with the character.

Echoes: beastmaster, hunter

GreenwardenResolve / Intellectnew

The druid proper. Plants, weather and healing, strongest far from walls.

Old Growth: +10 on checks in the wild; Growth and Life skills cost 20 % less.

Echoes: druid

SkinchangerVigor / Agilitynew

Takes a beast's shape when words run out. Teeth, claws, a nose that never lies.

Beast Form: once per fight, take a beast shape: +10 on physical checks, no spells or speech until it ends.

Echoes: shapeshifter

Craft

HearthwrightIntellect / Resolvekept

The maker. Brews, builds and mends, and turns shards and scraps into something useful.

Kitcraft: +10 on crafting checks; fuses shards from 2 fragments instead of 3.

Echoes: alchemist

RunesmithVigor / Intellectnew

Forge and inscription. Makes weapons and armour, then writes power into them.

Rune-Etch: once a day, inscribe a rune on an item: +5 on checks that use it until the next rest.

Echoes: smith-warrior, artificer

GearwrightIntellect / Agilitynew

Traps, devices and clever machines. Wins by preparing the ground.

Contraption: prepare one device per rest (trap, flare, lockbreaker, grapple); a placed device counts as a Terrain Overcome Condition.

Echoes: tinker, artificer

What changes, and what doesn't

  • Six classes stay (two with new stat pairs), Blade-sworn becomes Sellsword, Wager-bound is cut, and fifteen are new. Each primary stat leads at least three classes, so no stat is a dead end.
  • No gunpowder classes: Caldrenne has none.
  • Level 10 evolutions for every class come later, with the M3.5 content batches. A class-design skill would keep new classes and evolutions consistent: a checklist for role, stat pair, one trait of fixed size, god and affinity ties, data fields and tests.
  • Ddraig's game is at the Dual Offer, so he picks from the new list.
