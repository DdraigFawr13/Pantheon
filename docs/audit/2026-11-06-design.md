### F6 Attuned without the matching affinity is an unlisted canon question, and the story declared it first

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Evidence:** `docs/playtests/2026-11-06-m1-fight-check.md:8` "Canon does not say Attuned needs the affinity; noted for the owner."

### F7 Fights run past canon's 1 to 4 exchanges, the stated reason for counting hits

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** M
- **Evidence:** `docs/DECISIONS.md:240` "Counting hits keeps fights to the 1 to 4 exchanges canon asks for"
- **Case:** The one measured fight (a standard foe and a minion at 57 %) took 5 exchanges. A guarding foe cancels a plain success, and guard is 2 of 9 intent weights in `combat.yaml`. For short LitRPG sessions (D-046) that adds turns where no blow lands.
- **Fix:** Make exchange count a tuning item for the owner's fight playtest, with two candidate changes: standard foes take 1 hit, or guard only reduces hits after the first. Do not edit D-039 silently.

### F8 The pacing sim counts any 3 fragments as a shard, but fusing needs 3 of one affinity

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Evidence:** `scripts/pacing_sim.py:78` "run.fragments == 3"
- **Case:** Loot draws each fragment's affinity at random from the 15 basic ones unless the narrator names one, and fusing needs fragments of the same kind and target. The sim's first-shard line reports a shard far too early, and the owner reads it as the shard pace.
- **Fix:** Track fragments per affinity in the sim and report the first fusable trio; then decide whether fragments should follow the place's affinity by default.

### F9 The sim gives every fight 3 turns; the recorded fight took 5

- **Rework if left:** medium
- **Effort:** S
- **Evidence:** `scripts/pacing_sim.py:89` "turns a fight lasts"
- **Case:** Foes need 1 to 3 hits and failed rolls land none, so fights with several or elite foes last longer than 3 turns. Hours to Tier 2 come out optimistic. One two-foe fight at Level 1 gave two levels, against D-046's frequent small steps.
- **Fix:** Set fight length from the foes' hits and the check odds, or at least from the recorded fight; re-run the sim and update D-047's estimate.

### F10 The Tier 2 estimate skips the Ascension Trial and later tiers are never simulated
