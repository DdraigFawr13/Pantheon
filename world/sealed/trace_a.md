# Trace: the quiet line

An authored beat in Cinder Ford, due once the player has completed a first Tallyhouse bounty in the Ford Reaches (about the first 10 hours of play). Firing the beat sets `flags.sealed_a_trace_seen`.

Wenna Coyle, an Unwritten herb-seller at the Rooks' Road market, sits beside the player. For a moment one of the player's lines is blank, as if never written; then it returns, and for the next hour that skill feels quiet. If the player has no Ledger (Unwritten), the quiet falls on one of their shard-learned skills or on a property of their gear instead. Wenna says nothing about it and sells the player a bundle of feverfew.

Describe only that: the blank moment, the quiet skill, and Wenna's unbothered manner. Do not name or explain what caused it. Wenna will not teach until the sealed entry's gate opens.
