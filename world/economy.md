# Coin and prices

**Currency:** 12 copper (cp) = 1 silver (sp); 20 silver = 1 gold (gp). Hale mints the silver; Ironmarch mints most copper; gold is rare outside Saltmere and the courts.

| Item or service | Price |
|---|---|
| Ale, a mug | 1 cp |
| Bread, a loaf | 2 cp |
| A hot meal | 6 cp |
| A bed at an inn, one night | 1 sp |
| Rope, 15 m | 4 cp |
| Dagger | 3 sp |
| Sword | 15 sp |
| Chain shirt | 3 gp |
| Healing tonic (Common) | 2 sp |
| Tallyhouse registration | 5 sp |
| Tier 1 bounty, typical reward | 5 to 30 sp |
| Shard fragment (Common) | 1 gp |
| Skill or affinity shard (Common) | 5 gp |
| Skill or affinity shard (Uncommon) | 25 gp |
| Skill or affinity shard (Rare) | about 120 gp; rarely sold openly |

Shards above Rare are not sold; they are traded between houses, stolen, or fought over.
