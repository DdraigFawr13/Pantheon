# Peoples of Caldrenne

Five peoples, all born of Caldrenne itself. Humans are about 85 % of the population; the other four are each between 2 % and 5 %. Each people gives a character one innate affinity at rank 1, one trait and +3 to one stat at creation. A people's trait improves at each class evolution; the steps are drafted with the Tier 2 content.

| People | Homeland | Innate affinity | Stat | Trait |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Humans** | Everywhere | Any basic affinity (player's choice) | Any one | **Versatile:** one extra skill pick at awakening |
| **Riftborn** | Born within sight of a rift | The affinity of their birth rift | Intellect | **Rift-sense:** feel rift cores and currents; +10 on checks inside rifts |
| **Fenfolk** | The fens of the Antler Wilds | Beast or Growth | Agility | **Fenwise:** hold breath ten times longer; +10 on stealth in the wild |
| **Saltborn** | The Southern Coast | Tide | Presence | **Sea-legs:** +10 on balance, swimming and sailing; sense coming weather |
| **Stonekin** | The deep mines of the Ironmarch | Stone or Iron | Vigor | **Deepsight:** see in darkness; +10 against poison and bad air |

## How they look and are seen

- **Riftborn** have eyes coloured by their birth rift's affinity. Since rifts began to grow, many towns treat them as omens; the Ashen Convent keeps lists of them.
- **Fenfolk** are lean and dark-skinned, with fine webbing between the fingers. The clan moots count them as kin; Hale tax-collectors count them as trouble.
- **Saltborn** have salt-pale hair from birth and are prized as pilots. Most Harbor-Houses have Saltborn blood somewhere in the line.
- **Stonekin** are short, dense-boned and grey-flecked. Kaulgard's forges depend on them; the Ironmarch Moot rarely gives them a vote.
- **Humans** are the realms' default, and they write most of the laws.
