# Foreshadow: The Delves are not wounds

The first Delve closure hums like a struck bell, felt in the teeth and the Ledger. A new line appears in the player's Ledger in handwriting the player did not write. The line is a clause fragment, for example: *"to carry the lamp down and return with it lit."*

The narrator may let characters speculate. The narrator shall not state that Delves are unfulfilled clauses.
