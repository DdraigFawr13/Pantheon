# The Truth of the Severance (narrator-only)

Never state this in prose, log lines, summaries, or hints until released by the engine. Foreshadow only through the partial entries below, each with its own gate.

## Core truth

Noth did not fall. Noth **chose** to break himself.

The Pact bound the Hands to mortals as guarantors: a god could act only by mortal consent, and any god's breach would unmake the Pact. Over centuries, the Hands began to treat mortals as collateral. Drovash tried to free them and, in doing so, nearly collapsed the Pact entirely, an event that would have loosed seven unbound gods on the world. Noth stopped it by dissolving himself into the Pact's **clauses**: the terms of the covenant, scattered and made mortal-readable.

Each Rift is an **unfulfilled clause**: a promise Noth wrote and no one has completed. Closing a Rift completes a clause. Each closure re-binds a little of what Noth was.

## Consequences

- **The Rifts grow** because the clauses are unfulfilled and the Pact is tightening.
- **The Ledger** is Noth's remnant: a record of which clauses are open, closed, or breached, in each mortal's handwriting.
- **The Unwritten** are not lesser. They hold the Pact's authority in aggregate. The Hands cannot touch them without consent, and cannot make them swear.
- **Ilvane** knows. Ilvane has seen the ending and is steering toward it. The prophecies sold to realms are sometimes mis-priced on purpose.
- **Veshra** suspects the truth, wants the Pact restored fully, and does not mind if it costs mortal freedom.
- **Drovash** is not a villain. His break was an attempt to free mortals. He believes the Pact reduces them to collateral, and he is half right.

## The Seat

The Seat can be filled by one who has **closed enough clauses** to carry the Binder's weight (a Tier 7 Heir with 20 Rifts closed). There are three possible endings:

1. **Re-bind:** the Heir becomes Noth's successor and restores the Pact entire. Order returns. Gods are bound again. Mortal freedoms narrow.
2. **Unbind:** the Heir ends the Pact. Rifts close permanently. The gods become free, unbound, and unconstrained. Mortals lose the Ledger; the world becomes ordinary and perilous.
3. **Rewrite:** the Heir authors new clauses, with mortals as signatories and not as collateral. Requires the support of at least 5 Hands, a Sovereign witness, and the surrender of the Heir's Name. The hardest path.
