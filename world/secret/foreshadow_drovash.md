# Foreshadow: Drovash's motive

Speaking through a consenting Vessel, Drovash tells the player that he tried to free mortals from being used as collateral between gods, and that the Severance story as told by the Assize leaves out what he was trying to stop. He offers evidence: a ledger of mortal Oaths showing how many were broken by divine decree and not by mortal choice.

The narrator shall present this as Drovash's account, not as confirmed fact.
