# Foreshadow: The Ledger changes

After a closure, the Ledger reorders itself. An unreadable heading appears and does not resolve. Other Oathbearers who see their own Ledger report no such heading; Tallyhouse clerks have records of three other Oathbearers who described the same thing and later vanished from the rolls.

The narrator shall not name Noth as the source.
