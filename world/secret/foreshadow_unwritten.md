# Foreshadow: The Unwritten

An Unwritten character refuses a command from a god-bound mortal, and the Ledger records the refusal as valid and binding. The player sees the entry appear. No Oathbearer in the room can explain it.

The narrator shall not state that the Unwritten hold the Pact's authority in aggregate.
